# Contributing to Lambda Themes

We welcome contributions ! If you want to submit your own theme, please follow these guidelines:

## Submission Requirements
To make sure every theme fits nicely into the project, your pull request or contribution must include:

1. **A Clear Title :** Give your theme a unique and recognizable name.
2. **Custom Images :** All icons and assets must be your own work (hand-drawn or custom-made). Do not use copyrighted assets without permission.
3. **Profil :** Include your json theme.
4. **License :** You must include a license for your assets (e.g., CC BY-NC, MIT, etc.) so users know how they can use your work.
